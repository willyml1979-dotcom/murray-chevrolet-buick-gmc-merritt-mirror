This brand-new Crew Cab Pickup is powered by a robust Duramax 3.0L Turbo-Diesel I6 engine, delivering an impressive 305 hp and 495 lb-ft of torque. Paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission, this Sierra is ready to conquer any terrain with its four-wheel drive and off-road high clearance steps.

The AT4 Premium Package ensures you're traveling in style and comfort, with features like leather seats, a sunroof, and heated front seats for those chilly mornings.
