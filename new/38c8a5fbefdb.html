Step into the world of modern adventure with the 2026 Chevrolet Trax ACTIV in a striking Cacti Green. This sport utility vehicle is perfect for those who crave a blend of style, versatility, and cutting-edge features, making it an ideal choice for urban explorers and weekend warriors alike. With its turbocharged 1.2L 3-cylinder engine, this Trax delivers 137 horsepower and 162 lb-ft of torque, ensuring a spirited drive whether you're navigating city streets or hitting the open road.

The Trax ACTIV is packed with features that make every journey enjoyable and safe. Enjoy the open air and sunshine with the Sunroof Package, which includes a power sunroof and wireless charging. Inside, you'll find a comfortable and tech-savvy cabin with premium synthetic seats and a heated steering wheel for those cooler days. Smart Device Integration, Bluetooth Connection, and a WiFi Hotspot keep you connected, while the AM/FM Stereo and Satellite Radio provide endless entertainment options. The Trax ACTIV also offers practical features like keyless entry, remote engine start, and a spacious cargo area with a Cargo Shade for all your gear.
