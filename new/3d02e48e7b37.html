Step into the future with the 2026 GMC Acadia AT4, a commanding sport utility vehicle designed to conquer both the urban jungle and the great outdoors. Painted in a striking Ebony Twilight Metallic, this beauty is as captivating as a starry night sky. Under the hood, the Acadia AT4 roars with a 2.5L Turbocharged DOHC engine, delivering a formidable 328 horsepower and 326 lb-ft of torque. Paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive, this SUV ensures you're ready for any adventure.

Perfect for those who crave a blend of rugged capability and modern luxury, the Acadia AT4 is equipped with all-terrain tires, a tow hitch, and tow hooks for your off-road escapades. Meanwhile, the heated steering wheel, power liftgate, and remote start offer convenience at your fingertips, making this SUV as refined as it is powerful.

Safety is paramount in the Acadia AT4, featuring advanced systems like Lane Keeping Assist, Front Collision Mitigation, and a Blind Spot Monitor. The Aerial View Display System and Rear Parking Aid ensure you have an eagle's eye view of your surroundings, while the Adaptive Cruise Control and Evasion Assist keep your journeys smooth and stress-free.

Inside, the Acadia AT4 is a sanctuary of comfort with heated front seats, tri-zone climate control, and a premium sound system. Connectivity is seamless with Bluetooth, WiFi hotspot, and smart device integration. Whether you're commuting through town or embarking on a cross-country road trip, this SUV has you covered with its versatile three-row seating and spacious cargo area.
