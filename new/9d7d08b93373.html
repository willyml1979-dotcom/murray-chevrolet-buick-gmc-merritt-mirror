Get ready to experience the ultimate blend of luxury and performance with the 2026 GMC Canyon Denali Crew Cab Pickup. This brand-new Denali is all about rugged sophistication, making it the perfect partner for those who value both style and capability.

Beneath the hood lies the powerful Turbocharged Gas I4 2.7L engine, delivering a robust 310 hp and an impressive 430 lb-ft of torque. Paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, this truck promises smooth and responsive performance, whether you're cruising through the city or tackling off-road adventures.

The Denali trim is synonymous with premium features, and this Canyon is no exception. Enjoy the comfort of leather seats, a cooled front seat for those warm days, and the convenience of a power driver seat with memory settings. The sunroof with power inner sliding glass adds an open-air feel to your drive, while the heated steering wheel ensures comfort in cooler weather.

Safety and technology are at the forefront with features like Lane Departure Warning, Front Collision Mitigation, Blind Spot Monitor, and Adaptive Cruise Control. Stay connected with the smart device integration and a WiFi hotspot, while the premium sound system and satellite radio keep your entertainment options endless.

Style-wise, the Black Badging Package adds a unique, bold touch with black GMC emblems and badges. The exterior is further enhanced with custom-moulded splash guards and running boards, while the bed liner ensures protection for your cargo area.

With its four-wheel drive capability, Tow Hitch, and Locking/Limited Slip Differential, this Canyon Denali is ready for any adventure you throw its way.
