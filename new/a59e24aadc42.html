Meet the 2026 GMC Sierra 3500HD Denali, the ultimate workhorse with a touch of luxury. This Crew Cab Pickup is designed for those who need serious power and towing capability without compromising on comfort. With its Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine purring under the hood, delivering a whopping 470 horsepower and 975 lb-ft of torque, this beast is ready to tackle any job you throw its way. The 10-speed automatic transmission ensures smooth shifts and optimal performance, whether you're hauling heavy loads or cruising through the countryside.

Perfect for those who enjoy road trips with a trailer in tow, the Sierra 3500HD Denali comes equipped with a Gooseneck/5th Wheel Prep Package. This feature makes it easier than ever to connect your trailer and hit the open road. When it comes to comfort, this truck doesn't hold back, boasting heated and cooled leather seats, a power sunroof, and a heated steering wheel to make every drive feel like a first-class experience.

Stay connected and entertained with Bluetooth, a premium sound system, and a WiFi hotspot. Safety is paramount with features like adaptive cruise control, lane departure warning, and a blind spot monitor keeping you protected on every journey. For those who appreciate the finer things, the Sierra's sleek aluminum wheels, integrated tailgate step, and LED headlights offer both style and functionality.
