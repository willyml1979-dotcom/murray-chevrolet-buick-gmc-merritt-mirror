This brand-new sport utility vehicle is designed for those who refuse to compromise, offering both the ultimate in comfort and the capability to tackle any adventure. Under the hood, the impressive Duramax 3.0L Turbo-Diesel I6 engine delivers a robust 305 horsepower and a torque-rich 495 lb-ft, combined with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission. Whether you're cruising the highways or towing with confidence, the Max Trailering Package has you covered.

The High Country trim spares no luxury, featuring premium leather seats that are both heated and cooled in the front, and heated in the rear. Safety is paramount, with advanced features like Rear Collision Mitigation, Front Collision Warning, Adaptive Cruise Control, and a Blind Spot Monitor, ensuring peace of mind on every journey.

The Enhanced Trailer View system and Automatic Parking make maneuvering this full-sized SUV a breeze, while the WiFi Hotspot and Smart Device Integration keep you connected on the go. Stylish black bowtie emblems, luxurious liner protection, and sleek aluminum wheels add to the vehicle's striking appearance.
