Get ready to conquer the roads and off-road trails with the 2026 GMC Sierra 3500HD SLE Crew Cab Pickup, a powerhouse built for those who need strength and reliability in every mile. With its commanding Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine cranking out 470 horsepower and a whopping 975 lb-ft of torque, you'll have the muscle to tow, haul, and take on any challenge that comes your way. The 10-speed automatic transmission ensures smooth handling whether you're on the highway or navigating rugged terrain.

The Sierra 3500HD is loaded with features, including the X31 Off-Road and Protection Package that adds off-road suspension with Twin-tube Rancho shocks, Hill Descent Control, and skid plates for the transfer case and oil pan. Add a touch of luxury with the SLE Convenience Package, which offers dual climate control, a 10-way power driver seat, and LED fog lights. Plus, stay comfortable with the SLE Heated Package, featuring heated driver and passenger seats and a heated steering wheel.

This truck is as practical as it is powerful, featuring a Gooseneck/5th Wheel Prep Package for heavy-duty towing, a spray-on bedliner, and bed-mounted 7-pin trailer harness. The stylish 20"" bright face wheels with dark painted pockets and black badging package give the Sierra 3500HD a bold look, while the Coastal Dune color ensures you'll stand out from the crowd. Stay connected on the go with Bluetooth Connection, Satellite Radio, and a WiFi Hotspot.

Safety is paramount with front collision mitigation, lane departure warning, and a back-up camera to keep you aware of your surroundings. The Sierra 3500HD is the ultimate choice for those who need a truck that does it all, from work to play and everything in between.
