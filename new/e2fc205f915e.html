Meet the 2026 GMC Sierra 3500HD Pro Crew Cab Pickup a powerhouse on wheels ready to tackle the toughest tasks with style and strength. This beast under the hood boasts a formidable Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine, delivering an impressive 470 horsepower and a jaw-dropping 975 lb-ft of torque. Whether you're hauling loads across the prairies or navigating the rugged trails of the Rockies, this truck is your trusty partner.

The 10-speed automatic transmission ensures smooth gear changes, letting you focus on the journey ahead. With a gooseneck/5th wheel prep package, complete with hitch platform and spray-on bedliner, this Sierra is ready for your next big adventure, whether it's towing a trailer or heading off-road with the X31 Off-Road Package.

Inside, you'll find comfort and technology working in tandem. All-weather floor liners and color-keyed carpeting keep things tidy, while the rear bench seat offers plenty of room for your crew. Stay connected with Bluetooth, smart device integration, and a built-in WiFi hotspot. Safety features like front collision mitigation, lane departure warning, and automatic high beams provide peace of mind on every drive.

Rugged and resilient, this Sierra rolls on all-terrain tires and features a locking/limited slip differential for superior traction. Plus, with the added convenience of keyless entry, power windows, and an adjustable steering wheel, this truck is as practical as it is powerful.
