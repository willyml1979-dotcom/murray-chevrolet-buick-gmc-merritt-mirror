Looking for a sporty yet practical ride? The 2026 Chevrolet Trax 1RS is here to deliver! This brand-new sport utility vehicle is perfect for those seeking a balance of performance and comfort. Under the hood, you'll find a spirited ECOTEC 1.2L Turbocharged Gas 3-Cylinder engine, paired with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission. With 137 horsepower and 162 lb-ft of torque, this peppy engine is ready to tackle both urban streets and open highways with ease.

Safety and technology are at the forefront of the Trax 1RS. Enjoy peace of mind with advanced features like Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Departure Warning, and a Blind Spot Monitor. The Trax also comes equipped with Cross-Traffic Alert and a Rear Parking Aid, making it a breeze to maneuver in tight spaces. Stay connected on the go with Bluetooth, a WiFi Hotspot, and Smart Device Integration.

Comfort and convenience are not overlooked in this stylish SUV. Heated front seats and mirrors, a heated steering wheel, and remote engine start ensure you're prepared for any weather. The Trax also boasts power windows, cruise control, and a back-up camera for added ease. With its aluminum wheels and rear spoiler, this vehicle isn't just functional it's also fun to look at!
