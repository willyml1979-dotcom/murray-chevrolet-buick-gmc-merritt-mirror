Meet the 2022 Chrysler Pacifica Touring, a stylish and family-friendly minivan that's ready to make your journeys more comfortable and enjoyable. This Pacifica comes with the S Appearance Package, which adds a sleek touch with its black daylight opening moldings and premium black rear fascia. The anodized ink badging and 18"" aluminum wheels further enhance its bold look.

This minivan is powered by a robust 3.6L Pentastar VVT V6 engine paired with a smooth 9-speed automatic transmission, delivering both efficiency and performance. With all-wheel drive capability, the Pacifica is prepared to tackle any road condition, making it perfect for family trips across the seasons.

Inside, you'll find a spacious and luxurious cabin with black cloth bucket seats featuring ravine inserts, ensuring comfort for every passenger. The Pacifica is packed with tech features including a Bluetooth connection, satellite radio, and smart device integration, making sure everyone stays entertained and connected on the go.

Safety is paramount with features like adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitor, lane keeping assist, and cross-traffic alert to give you peace of mind during your travels. The heated front seats and steering wheel provide added comfort, especially during colder months.

With relatively low mileage and a host of premium features, this Chrysler Pacifica Touring is the perfect blend of style, comfort, and practicality. Whether you're navigating city streets or heading out for a long road trip, this minivan is equipped to handle it all.
