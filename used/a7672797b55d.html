Step into the realm of luxury and versatility with this 2015 Dodge Durango Citadel. Perfectly designed for families and those who crave adventure, this sport utility vehicle is an ideal companion for road trips and daily drives around the region. With its relatively low mileage, this Durango promises plenty of future road trip memories.

At the heart of this beauty is the robust 3.6L Pentastar VVT V6 engine, paired with an 8-Speed TorqueFlite Automatic transmission. This powertrain ensures a smooth and powerful drive, whether you're cruising through the city or heading off the beaten path.

The Durango Citadel doesn't just perform well; it pampers its passengers with an array of luxurious features. Enjoy the comfort of Nappa leather-faced seats, heated front and rear seats, and a heated steering wheel, perfect for those chilly mornings. The rear DVD entertainment center will keep passengers entertained on long journeys, and the premium sound system ensures that your favorite tunes sound crystal clear.

This SUV is equipped with the latest technology, including adaptive cruise control, forward collision warning, and blind-spot detection, making it a safe choice for your family. The Uconnect 8.4"" touchscreen with GPS navigation and Bluetooth connectivity keeps you connected and on the right path.

With the Trailer Tow Group IV package, this Durango is ready to haul, featuring a rear load-leveling suspension and a Class IV hitch receiver. Plus, the sun/moonroof and rear A/C add to the comfort and enjoyment for all passengers.

Don't miss out on this blend of luxury, performance, and practicality, all wrapped in a bright white exterior that turns heads wherever it goes.
